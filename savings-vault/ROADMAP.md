# The Roadmap — Plain Version

Ten steps, in order. Each one has a pass mark. If you pass, keep going. If you fail,
you stop and think — having lost a weekend instead of a year.

---

## First: the money, cleared up

There are **three different versions** of this app. All prices in South African Rand.

| Version | Cost | What it does | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Notebook** | **R5,000** | Never touches anyone's money. You log what you almost bought, it counts it up, once a week it reminds you to move it yourself. | **You build this** |
| The Mover | R80,000+ | Moves money from your account to your *own* savings account automatically. | Maybe year 3 |
| The Real Vault | R500,000+ | Actually holds people's savings. Only legal with a licensed bank partner. | Probably never, or age 22 |

**You need about R5,000. That's it.** The R500,000 was me explaining why you should *not*
build that version — not something you have to find. If you ever spend it, it'll be years
away, and by then the app will be earning or investors will be paying.

---

## Second: about the "100% chance"

There is no 100%. Nobody can give you that. Anyone who says they can is selling something.

But here's what's actually better than it sounds: this plan doesn't guarantee you win.
It guarantees that **if you're going to lose, you find out in two weeks instead of two
years** — and it costs almost nothing when you do.

Most people who fail at business didn't fail because the idea was bad. They failed because
they spent three years and their savings before checking whether anyone wanted it.

## The ten steps

### 01 — The 20 People Test · 2 weeks · R0 · **DO THIS FIRST**

**Do this:** Make a WhatsApp group. Add 20 friends and family. Tell them: *"For the next
2 weeks — every time you nearly buy something you don't need and you stop yourself, send a
message here. What it was, how much. That's all."*

**Why it matters more than anything else:** Sending a WhatsApp is easier than opening an
app. If people won't do the easy version for free, they will never do the hard version.

- **Passed if:** 10+ of the 20 send a message in week one, without nagging.
- **Stop if:** fewer than 10. Don't build the app. You just saved a year and R5,000.

### 02 — The Sign-Up Page · same 2 weeks · R0

**Do this:** One page on Carrd (free). The name (Skipped), one line — "Save the money you
almost spent" — and an email box. Link it in your TikTok bio.

**Why:** Those emails are who you launch to. Without this you launch to nobody.

- **Passed if:** 100 emails before you start building.

### 03 — Start Posting, Never Stop · today · R0 · forever

**Do this:** One TikTok a day. *"Day 1 of building a savings app. I'm 16 and I have no
idea what I'm doing yet."*

**Why:** No money for ads means this is your only way to get users. And your story — 16,
building in public — is the one thing a competitor with money can't copy.

Your first 30 videos will be bad. Post them anyway.

- **Passed if:** you posted 30 days in a row.

### 04 — The Adult Conversation · 1 afternoon · R500

**Do this:** Sit down with a parent or trusted adult. You need three things:
- Register a company. **They** are the director — you can't be one until 18.
- Apple developer account in their name (same reason).
- **In writing: you own the shares.**

**Why the third is the important one:** Director is a job title. **Shares are the part
worth money.** You can legally own them at 16. Write it down now, while the company is
worth nothing and everyone is happy.

- **Passed if:** you have a signed piece of paper saying you own the company.

### 05 — Build the Simple App · 6 weeks · R2,500

**The app does exactly three things:**
- A big **+** button. Type the amount. Tap. Done in under 3 seconds.
- A big number showing everything skipped so far.
- Once a week: "You skipped R380. Go move it." With a button opening their banking app.

**The app does NOT** touch anyone's money. Ever. That's what keeps it legal, cheap and
safe — and it isn't a limitation, it's the reason you can build this at all.

**Why so small:** every extra feature is another month and another thing that breaks.

- **Passed if:** three friends use it for a week without you reminding them.

### 06 — The Safety Check · 1 day · R0 · before launch

**Three questions, answer all three yourself:**
- **Can one user see another user's stuff?** Log in as you, then try to open a friend's
  data by changing the number in the address. If you can, so can everyone.
- **Are any passwords or keys hidden inside the app?** They shouldn't be — anything inside
  the app can be read by anyone who downloads it.
- **Does "delete my account" really delete everything?** Apple requires it. So does SA law.

**The rule about AI-built code:** Claude can write your code. But never let it write a
safety feature you don't understand. Ask for it in plain English until you get it. If you
can't explain to a friend how your app stops one person reading another's data, you don't
know that it does.

- **Passed if:** you can explain all three answers out loud, to a person, without notes.

### 07 — Launch · 2 weeks · R2,000

App Store and Google Play. Email your sign-up list. Post the launch video. Apple takes 1–3
days to review and may reject it the first time — that's normal. They say why, you fix it,
you resend.

- **Passed if:** 500 downloads.

### 08 — Make People Stay · 3 months · R0

**Do this:** Stop chasing new users entirely. For three months your only job is stopping
the ones you have from leaving. Message people who stopped and ask why. Then fix that.

**Why this is the real test:** normally only 4 of every 100 people who download a money app
are still using it a month later. That's the industry, not bad luck.

- **Passed if:** 15 of every 100 still using it after a month (nearly 4× normal).

### 09 — Start Charging · 3 months · R0

**Free forever:** logging, one goal, weekly reminder. Never take these away — this builds
the habit.

**R40/month:** multiple goals, home-screen widget, "what this becomes in 5 years", saving
with a friend, full history.

Two things that matter a lot:
- Give a **30-day** free trial, not 3 days. Long trials convert ~70% better.
- Show the price **after** they see their first big total, not on first open.

- **Passed if:** 3 of every 100 users pay.

### 10 — Decide · month 12

10,000 users at 3% paying = **R12,000/month.** You're 17. Real money, and real proof.

Now — and only now — you can talk to a bank or an investor about the actual vault. Not
with an idea. With numbers.

---

## Four rules, the whole way through

1. **Never hold anyone's money.** Not R1. Not "just for a second." This is the line
   between a side project and a crime.
2. **Never ask for someone's banking password.** No screen, no reason, ever.
3. **Never skip a pass mark.** They exist so failure is cheap.
4. **Never ship code you can't explain.**

## What winning actually looks like

| People paying | Per month | What that means |
|---|---|---|
| 100 | R4,000 | Proof it works. You're 17. |
| 1,000 | R40,000 | More than most graduates earn. |
| **10,000** | **R400,000** | **A real company. You're 19.** |

Aim at 100. Then 1,000. Nobody reached 100,000 without passing through 100 first.
